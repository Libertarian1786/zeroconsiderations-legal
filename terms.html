3. What the app is — and what it is not

Everything the app produces is advisory. Zero Considerations estimates shot-group geometry from photographs and suggests sight adjustments using standard ballistic mathematics. Those numbers are estimates, not results.

They depend on photo quality, camera angle, lighting, the scale reference you provide, the optic specifications you select, and ballistic inputs that vary between firearms, barrels, and ammunition lots. The app never certifies a zero and never tells you a firearm is zeroed.

You must confirm every adjustment the app suggests by firing live rounds at your actual target distance before you rely on it. Never assume a firearm is zeroed because this app predicted that it would be.

The app is a measurement and calculation tool. It does not control, modify, or interact with any firearm. It does not sell, list, broker, or link to firearms, ammunition, or any other regulated item.

Not a substitute for instruction

This app is not a substitute for qualified, in-person firearms instruction. No software can replace a certified instructor, range discipline, or your own judgement. If you are new to firearms, get proper instruction before you shoot. Nothing in the app or on this site is training, certification, or professional advice of any kind.

Built-in data has limits too

Optic click values, reticle geometry, ballistic presets, and published muzzle velocities in the app are compiled from manufacturers' own publications and other public sources. They may not match your specific unit, revision, barrel length, or ammunition lot. Verify click values against your own turret on paper, and chronograph your own loads when precision matters.
