Privacy Policy — Zero Considerations

Effective August 10, 2026 (supersedes July 20, 2026)

Zero Considerations ("the app", "we") is a firearm zeroing and ballistics tool that runs on your device. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information. The short version: every measurement and every ballistic calculation happens on your phone, we do not collect, sell, or share your personal information, and the app makes exactly one outbound request — the optional weather auto-fill you tap.

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not require an account, name, email, or login to use the app.
  • We do not use advertising networks.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you create in the app — your firearms, optics, mounts, loads, zeroing sessions, photos of your targets, and results — is stored only on your device. We do not upload it or have any access to it. Photos you take for shot-group measurement are processed entirely on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Optional weather auto-fill (the one outbound request)

The app has an optional feature that fills in environmental conditions (temperature, pressure, humidity) for your shooting location. If — and only if — you tap it, the app sends your device's approximate location (latitude and longitude, rounded to four decimal places, with no name, account, or device identifier attached) to a third-party weather service to retrieve current conditions. We do not receive or store that location — the request goes from your phone to the weather service directly, and the answer is used only to fill those fields.

The weather service is MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute) in current versions of the app; earlier versions used Open-Meteo. Each service's handling of the request is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. If you don't use weather auto-fill, the app makes no network request at all — every measurement, photo analysis, and ballistic solution works with the phone in airplane mode.

The app store, and links you tap

  • Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details — we receive only anonymous, aggregate sales counts from the store.
  • The "rate this app" prompt uses Apple's and Google's own in-app review APIs. Your review goes to the store, not to us, and we cannot see who left which review or tie a review to a person.
  • Links to a manufacturer's manual open that manufacturer's website in your own browser. Once you leave the app, that site's privacy practices apply, not ours.

Optional feedback you choose to send

If you email us a feature idea, a bug report, or a complaint, we receive what you send us (and any contact information you choose to include). We use it solely to respond to you and to improve the app. We do not use it for advertising and do not share it. Sending feedback is always your choice; the app does not send anything on your behalf.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, uninstalling the app removes it. If you sent us feedback and would like it deleted, email us at the address below and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
