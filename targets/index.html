How to print zeroing targets at true scale

  • In your print dialog, set scale to 100% / "Actual size" — turn OFF "fit to page" and "shrink to printable area."
  • After printing, measure the 1-inch bar with a ruler: it must be exactly 25.4 mm. If it isn't, your printer scaled the page.
  • Letter (8.5×11") for US printers; A4 for everywhere else — both versions are dimensioned identically where it matters.

Why the corner squares? They're ArUco registration markers. Photograph your shot-up target with the Zero Considerations app and the markers set the photo's scale and orientation automatically — the app measures your group and computes the turret clicks for your optic, no walking back and forth with a ruler.
