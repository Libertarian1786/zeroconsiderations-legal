Long (about 100 words)

Zero Considerations is a firearm zeroing and ballistics app for iPhone and Android. Photograph your target after a group and it finds the holes, measures the group against your point of aim, and gives the exact clicks for your optic. $9.99 once, no subscription. The catalog behind it carries 1,043 optic entries carrying the makers' own click values, 852 firearms, 512 loads and 301 reticle patterns drawn to the makers' published subtension sheets wherever the maker publishes the numbers. It works entirely offline — no account, no ads, no analytics — with the single exception of an optional one-tap weather lookup for temperature, humidity and pressure when a signal is available. Free printable sight-in targets are posted at zeroconsiderations.com/targets/ whether or not you buy the app. It is built and maintained by one developer, with no company or team behind it.

Founder bio

David McCoy is a travel nurse who built Zero Considerations between hospital assignments over about a year, after Strelok Pro left the app stores in the sanctions fallout and nothing replaced its reticle library. He is the app's only developer.
