Hornady 4DOF Is Free and Good. Here's What It Doesn't Do.

Any honest "alternative" page has to open this way: the Hornady app is free, polished, and backed by a real ballistics lab, with one of the largest rating bases in the category (tens of thousands of ratings, around 4.8 stars as of this writing). Its 4DOF solver is serious work. If a free trajectory table is the whole job, install it and keep your ten dollars.

So why does this page exist? Because "free from an ammo maker" is a business model, and the app is shaped by it — and because the job it's not shaped for is the one that costs most shooters the most ammo every year.

Who's writing this: the developer of Zero Considerations — one of the apps below. Weigh accordingly. Competitor facts are taken from their public store listings and sites as of September 2026, and where an app is the better pick for a job, the page says so.

What the free app is for

Hornady's app exists to make Hornady ammunition perform for you — a fair trade, openly made. Its solver leans on Hornady's measured drag data, which is excellent when you shoot supported bullets, and it recently added a group-analysis feature, a sign the company knows where the demand is. What the model doesn't reward is deep support for every maker's optics, reticles, and loads — or the unglamorous mechanics of sight-in day.

The gap: sight-in day

  • Holes to clicks. Zero Considerations photographs your shot target once, finds the target face and every hole automatically, and converts the group's offset into the exact click count for your optic's click value and turret direction. Dial it, confirm with a group.
  • Truing from paper. A photographed group at known distance backs out your true muzzle velocity and BC — no chronograph, any maker's ammunition.
  • Reticles to scale. 215 reticle patterns drawn from published manufacturer subtension data, so holdovers are something you can see, not just a number.
  • Your whole rig. 793 firearm, 596 optic, and 512 factory-load presets across makers, height-over-bore computed from your optic and mount, offset optics and lasers each with their own zero.

The honest recommendation

This isn't replace-it advice; it's add-the-missing-half advice. Run Hornady's solver if you like it. The photograph-to-clicks loop, the truing, and the reticle library are the half the free ecosystem doesn't cover — and that half is a one-time $9.99, not another subscription.
